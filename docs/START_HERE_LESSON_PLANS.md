# Start Here: lesson plans (absolute beginners)

Content lives in [`curriculum/stage-0.json`](../curriculum/stage-0.json). This stage comes first in the course and replaces the original "Alphabet & Sounds" unit, which introduced 10 letters per lesson with no sound or explanation.

## Teaching approach

- **Speaking first.** The first two units teach useful phrases through *pronunciation spelling* (romanized Urdu) shown large, with the Urdu script small underneath. Learners can talk from day one, and they get used to seeing the script without having to read it.
- **Teach, then test.** Every new item gets a "New word" or "New letter" card first (meaning, picture, sound), then a question on it straight away. This now applies to the whole course, not just this stage.
- **Small steps.** 3–5 new items per lesson. Any lesson anywhere in the course with more than 6 new items is automatically split into parts.
- **Letters build real words.** Each alphabet lesson adds 3–4 letters and immediately reads 2–3 words made only of letters already taught (ا ب پ → آپ, باپ). Many of them are words the learner already *said* in Units 1–2.
- **Only known words as wrong answers.** Multiple-choice options come from the current lesson or earlier lessons wherever possible.
- **Review lessons** (marked `"review": true`) revisit a unit's items without re-teaching them.

Lesson-level options in the JSON: `"mode": "speaking"` (pronunciation spelling as the main text) and `"review": true` (no teaching cards).

## Lessons

### Unit 1: Start Here: First Words

_Say hello, thank you and introduce yourself_

| # | Lesson | New items | Goals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hello! | assalam-o-alaikum, wa alaikum assalam, shukriya, khuda hafiz | Greet someone and reply to a greeting; Say thank you and goodbye |
| 2 | Yes, No & Sorry | jee, nahin, theek hai, maaf kijiye, koi baat nahin | Say yes and no politely; Apologise and say 'no problem' |
| 3 | How Are You? | aap kaise hain?, aap kaisi hain?, main theek hoon, aur aap?, alhamdulillah | Ask how someone is (to a man or a woman); Say you're fine and ask back |
| 4 | My Name Is... | mera naam, mera naam Ali hai., aap ka naam kya hai?, aap se mil kar khushi hui. | Say your name; Ask someone's name; Say 'nice to meet you' |
| 5 | Review: My First Conversation | (review) assalam-o-alaikum, shukriya, aap kaise hain?, aap ka naam kya hai?, khuda hafiz | Hold a short conversation from hello to goodbye |

### Unit 2: Start Here: Everyday Words

_Family, food and drink, and counting to five_

| # | Lesson | New items | Goals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family | ammi, abbu, bhai, behan, yeh meri ammi hain. | Name close family members; Introduce a family member |
| 2 | Food & Drink | paani, chai, roti, doodh, mujhe paani chahiye. | Name everyday food and drinks; Ask for something politely |
| 3 | Counting to Five | ek, do, teen, chaar, paanch | Count from one to five; Order two of something |
| 4 | Review: At the Tea Stall | (review) chai, do, paani, shukriya, bhai | Order tea politely, from greeting to thanks |

### Unit 3: Start Here: Reading the Alphabet

_The Urdu letters, three or four at a time_

| # | Lesson | New items | Goals |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First Letters: ا ب پ | ا, ب, پ, آپ, باپ | Recognise ا ب پ and their sounds; Read your first words: آپ and باپ; Know that Urdu reads right to left |
| 2 | Letters: ت ٹ ث | ت, ٹ, ث, بات, تب | Recognise ت ٹ ث; Hear the difference between soft ت and hard ٹ; Read بات and تب |
| 3 | Letters: ج چ خ | ج, چ, خ, چاچا, تاج | Recognise ج چ خ; Read چاچا and تاج |
| 4 | Letters: د ڈ ر | د, ڈ, ر, دادا, رات | Recognise د ڈ ر; Know that these letters never join to the next letter; Read دادا and رات |
| 5 | Letters: ز س ش | ز, س, ش, سات, بس, شاباش | Recognise ز س ش; Read سات, بس and شاباش |
| 6 | Letters: ک گ ل | ک, گ, ل, لال, کالا, گاجر | Recognise ک گ ل; Read colour words لال and کالا |
| 7 | Letters: م ن و | م, ن, و, نام, آم, نو | Recognise م ن و; Know that و can be a consonant (w/v) or a vowel (oo/o); Read نام and آم |
| 8 | Letters: ی ے ہ | ی, ے, ہ, یہ, ہے, بیٹا | Recognise ی ے ہ; Read the everyday words یہ, ہے and بیٹا |
| 9 | Letters: ف ق غ | ف, ق, غ, فون, قلم | Recognise ف ق غ; Practise the deep sounds ق and غ; Read فون and قلم |
| 10 | Letters: ں ھ ڑ | ں, ھ, ڑ, نہیں, گھر, بڑا | Recognise ں ھ ڑ; Read the essential words نہیں, گھر and بڑا |
| 11 | Same Sound, Different Letter: h, s, t | ح, ص, ط, حال, صاف, طوطا | Recognise ح ص ط; Understand that some letters share a sound with letters you know; Read حال, صاف and طوطا |
| 12 | Same Sound, Different Letter: z and ع | ذ, ض, ظ, ع, عید, ضرور | Recognise ذ ض ظ ع; Read عید, ضرور and ذرا; Finish the alphabet! |

## Recording priority

Speaking-first lessons depend on audio more than anything else. Record the **Start Here** rows at the top of [audio-todo.md](assets/audio-todo.md) first: about 60 short clips cover every lesson in this stage.
